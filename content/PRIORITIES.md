## What visitors do (rankings only)

- Most visitors arrive from **Facebook** (mostly the mobile app) and land on the **home page**; direct/WhatsApp-style visits are second, Google search is still a small but growing share.
- **Mobile is the large majority** — every new page must be checked on a phone first.
- After the home page, the most visited pages are: the **tests hub** (/tests/), the **«شريكي محيّرني» partner series** (/partner/), the about pages (people check who the author is), /questions/, and the English home (/en/).
- Most-read single article: **«تكرار العلاقات المؤذية: ليه بتختار نفس الشخص كل مرة؟»**, then «الفرق بين الحزن والاكتئاب».
- Most-used tests: **PHQ-8 (depression)**, **attachment style (ECR-RS)**, GAD-7.
- Topic groups by interest: (1) toxic/difficult partners & repeating patterns, (2) attachment styles, (3) depression vs sadness, (4) anxiety. Videos, glossary and worksheets get little direct traffic so far.
