About

Dr. Millad Mikhail holds a Ph.D. in Mental Health from Suez Canal University, Egypt, and is a member of the American Psychological Association (APA). His doctoral research examined how logo counselling, the meaning-centred approach of Viktor Frankl, can reduce existential anxiety and strengthen psychological immunity among university students.

He creates Arabic-language mental health education content on his YouTube channel, Glset Shezlong, and is the author of «ليه بنحب اللي بيوجعنا؟» (Why Do We Love What Hurts Us?), a book on attachment and harmful relationships.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Dr. Millad Mikhail?

Dr. Millad Mikhail (د. ميلاد ميخائيل) is a mental health educator and author. He holds a Ph.D. in Mental Health from Suez Canal University, Egypt (2022), and is a member of the American Psychological Association (APA). He wrote the book «ليه بنحب اللي بيوجعنا؟» (Why Do We Love What Hurts Us?) and creates Arabic mental health content on the YouTube channel جلسة شيزلونج (Glset Shezlong).

Is Dr. Milad Mounir the same person as Dr. Millad Mikhail?

Yes. His full name is Millad Mounir Hakim Bekheet Mikhail. In Arabic he is known as د. ميلاد منير and د. ميلاد ميخائيل; in English as Dr. Millad Mikhail and Milad Mounir.

What are his qualifications?

A Ph.D. in Mental Health from Suez Canal University (2022); a Master's degree in Education, Mental Health, from Suez Canal University (2018); a Specialized Diploma in Counseling and Clinical Psychology from Tanta University (2021); and a Special Diploma and a General Diploma in Education from Suez Canal University. His credentials were evaluated by World Education Services (WES). He is currently taking graduate psychology coursework as a visiting graduate student at the JFK School of Psychology, National University (USA).

What was his doctoral research about?

His dissertation, “Effectiveness of Logo Counselling on Reducing the Level of Existential Anxiety and Improving Psychological Immunity for University Students”, was completed at the Mental Health Department, Faculty of Education, Suez Canal University (2022).

What books has he written?

«ليه بنحب اللي بيوجعنا؟», a practical guide to attachment styles and hurtful relationships, available on Amazon in Arabic and English. A second book, «بطاريتي 1%» (My Battery Is at 1%), is coming soon.

Where can I follow his work?

On this website (articles and free self-tests), the YouTube channel جلسة شيزلونج, the blog راحة البال at blog.drmilladmikhail.com, LinkedIn, and his Amazon author page.
