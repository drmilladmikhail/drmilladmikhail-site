About Dr. Millad Mikhail, PhD

Mental health educator and author writing in Arabic about attachment, relationships, anxiety and meaning.

Dr. Millad Mikhail, PhD · د. ميلاد ميخائيل

Also known in Arabic media as د. ميلاد منير (Dr. Milad Mounir).

Biography

Dr. Millad Mikhail holds a Ph.D. in Mental Health from the Department of Mental Health, Faculty of Education in Ismailia, Suez Canal University (Egypt, 2022). His doctoral research studied the effectiveness of logo counselling (meaning-centred counselling) in reducing existential anxiety and improving psychological immunity among university students.

His qualifications have been evaluated by World Education Services (WES), and he is a member of the American Psychological Association (APA). He is currently taking graduate psychology courses as a visiting student at the JFK School of Psychology, National University (USA).

He is the author of Why Do We Love What Hurts Us? (also in Arabic), and he hosts the Arabic YouTube channel «جلسة شيزلونج» (Glset Shezlong). On this website he publishes free Arabic articles, self-tests and worksheets.
